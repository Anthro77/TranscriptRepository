Limbo especially stands out to me. The souls there say they live “without hope yet in desire.” Is Dante suggesting that civilisation based on reason alone — like Greece and Rome — can reach dignity but not completion? Another thing I noticed: the souls in Hell do not repent, change, or leave, yet Dante himself clearly changes while travelling through Hell alive. So is Inferno really describing punishment, or is it showing what happens when the human will becomes fixed permanently?
