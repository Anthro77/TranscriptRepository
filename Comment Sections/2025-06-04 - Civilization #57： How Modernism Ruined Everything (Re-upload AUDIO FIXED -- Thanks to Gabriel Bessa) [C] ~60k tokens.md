*Changes in Freud: Freudian theory underwent constant changes and adjustments, as it was a new field. In 1920, he developed the concept of the life and death instincts, placing the libido exclusively within the framework of the life instinct.

*Child sexual abuse: In both men and women, Freud and his collaborators found the same original sexual trauma—that of seduction by an adult. Hence, having ruled out the possibility of actual sexual abuse, he initially confined it to the realm of fantasy. ### **Freud's Early Work on Hysteria (1896)**

- ✅ **TRUE**: Freud did initially propose the "seduction theory" - that hysteria was caused by childhood sexual abuse
- ✅ **TRUE**: He wrote "The Aetiology of Hysteria" in 1896 arguing this position
- ✅ **TRUE**: He later abandoned this theory
- ✅ **TRUE**: He shifted to emphasizing fantasy and internal drives over actual abuse

### **The Oedipus and Electra Complexes**

- ✅ **TRUE**: Freud developed the Oedipus complex theory
- ⚠ **PARTIALLY TRUE**: The "Electra complex" was actually named by Carl Jung, not Freud. Freud used "feminine Oedipus complex" but didn't fully embrace Jung's terminology

### **Freud and Carl Jung's Relationship**

- ✅ **TRUE**: Jung was Freud's protégé and heir apparent
- ✅ **TRUE**: They had a major falling out (around 1913)
- ✅ **TRUE**: Freud was protective of his theories and didn't like challenges
- ❌ **EXAGGERATION**: "Excommunicated" is dramatic language, but they did break ties

### **Jung's Theories**

- ✅ **TRUE**: Jung developed concepts of collective unconscious, archetypes, persona, shadow, animus/anima
- ✅ **TRUE**: Jung popularized introvert/extrovert personality types

### **Semmelweis Story**

- ✅ **TRUE**: Ignaz Semmelweis discovered handwashing prevented childbed fever in 1840s Vienna
- ✅ **TRUE**: His findings were rejected by the medical establishment
- ✅ **TRUE**: He was institutionalized and died shortly after (1865)
- ⚠ **DETAILS UNCLEAR**: Whether he was "beaten to death by guards" is debated - he may have died from infection after a beating, or from other causes

## ❌ **What's FALSE or Highly Misleading**

- ❌ **DISPUTED**: Masson's interpretation is **highly controversial** and rejected by most Freud scholars
- ❌ **OVERSIMPLIFIED**: The reasons Freud abandoned seduction theory are complex and debated

### **Why Freud Changed His Theory**

**The lecture claims:** Freud changed to protect wealthy clients (fathers who were abusers)

**Reality is more complex:**

- ⚠ Freud's own letters suggest he found inconsistencies in patient accounts
- ⚠ He came to believe some memories were fantasies, not all
- ⚠ He never completely abandoned the idea that real abuse could occur
- ⚠ The timeline and reasons are debated by historians - financial pressure is ONE theory, not proven fact

### **Frankism in Vienna**

- ✅ **TRUE**: Frankism was a real Jewish religious movement following Sabbatai Zevi
- ⚠ **UNCLEAR**: Connection to widespread sexual abuse in Vienna is speculative
- ❌ **WEAK LINK**: Using this as evidence that Freud's patients were telling the truth is a logical leap
