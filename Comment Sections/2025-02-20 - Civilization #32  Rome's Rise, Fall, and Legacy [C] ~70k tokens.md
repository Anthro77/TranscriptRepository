Thus, if Proj Jiang can so irresponsibly assert that "immigration caused the fall of the Western Roman Empire," an equally irresponsible historical commentator could just as easily say, "the mistreatment and disenfranchisement of migrants in the WRE is what led to its fall."

That is the main problem with these ridiculous parallelisms between Ancient Rome and U.S./European history--Roman history was so broad and occurred so long ago under such drastically different circumstances to the modern era that any armchair historian in the present day can pick and choose facts to justify just about any political narrative or interpretation they're trying to make (and it will sound plausible to impressionable teenagers and the casual YouTube viewer). 2000 years from now, there will be commentators saying, "The abolition of slavery in the U.S. and the passage of the 13th amendment directly led to the eventual collapse of America, and this is why slavery is justified."
